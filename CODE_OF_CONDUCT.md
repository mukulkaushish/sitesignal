# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SiteSignal's community should be a welcoming place to learn, contribute, ask
questions, and disagree constructively. Participation is open regardless of
experience level, background, identity, nationality, appearance, disability,
religion, or personal circumstances.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive information from public reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while discussing tradeoffs
  constructively.
- Help keep discussions focused and accessible to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, publication of private information, and personal attacks
are not acceptable. Spam, malicious code, intentionally misleading security
reports, and retaliation against good-faith reporters are also prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the SiteSignal project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using a contact method on
the repository owner's GitHub profile. If no private maintainer channel is
available, use GitHub's blocking and abuse-reporting tools without publishing
private details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads,
reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and avoid
conflicts of interest when reviewing them.
